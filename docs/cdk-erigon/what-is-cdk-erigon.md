# What is CDK-Erigon?

CDK-Erigon is a high-performance fork of [Erigon Ethereum client](https://github.com/erigontech/erigon), modified specifically to work as a sequencer and RPC node for Polygon zkEVM protocol blockchains.

## Development

Developed by the Gateway R&D team over an 18-month period, CDK-Erigon represents a significant engineering effort focused on performance optimization and usability improvements. The team has meticulously enhanced the original Erigon codebase to meet the specific requirements of zkEVM networks.

## Production Use

The node implementation serves as the backbone for Gateway's L2 deployment offerings, and is the default node of choice in their [Presto platform](https://gateway.fm/presto). Its proven reliability and performance in production environments make it an ideal choice for operators requiring stable and efficient blockchain infrastructure.

## Key Advantages

- Specialized for Polygon zkEVM protocol
- Production-hardened by Gateway's engineering team
- Optimized for both sequencer and RPC node operations
- Built on Erigon's efficient architecture
- Continuously maintained and improved
