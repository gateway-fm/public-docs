# CL Nodes

Consensus Layer (CL) nodes are essential for maintaining the consensus of the Ethereum network. They are responsible for validating blocks, participating in consensus, and ensuring the network's security and integrity. Here are some of the most popular CL nodes:

- **Lighthouse**: A fast, secure, and efficient Ethereum consensus client developed by Sigma Prime. It is known for its performance and security features.
- **Lodestar**: Developed by ChainSafe, Lodestar is a TypeScript-based Ethereum consensus client that focuses on modularity and ease of use.
- **Nimbus**: A nimble and resource-efficient Ethereum consensus client developed by Status, designed to run on resource-constrained devices.
- **Prysm**: Developed by Prysmatic Labs, Prysm is a Go-based Ethereum consensus client known for its robustness and extensive feature set.
- **Teku**: Developed by ConsenSys, Teku is a Java-based Ethereum consensus client designed for enterprise use, offering advanced features and scalability.

Each of these clients has its own strengths and is suitable for different scenarios, depending on the specific needs of the network participants.
