# EL Nodes

Execution Layer (EL) nodes are a critical component of the Ethereum network, responsible for processing transactions, executing smart contracts, and maintaining the state of the blockchain. Here are some of the most popular EL nodes:

- **Besu**: An open-source Ethereum client designed for enterprise use, offering advanced privacy features and permissioning capabilities.
- **Erigon**: Known for its high performance and low resource consumption, Erigon is a re-implementation of Ethereum that focuses on efficiency and scalability.
- **Geth**: The most widely used Ethereum client, Geth is known for its robustness and extensive feature set, making it a popular choice for both developers and enterprises.
- **Nethermind**: A .NET-based Ethereum client that offers fast sync times and a modular architecture, making it suitable for a variety of use cases.

Each of these clients has its own strengths and is suitable for different scenarios, depending on the specific needs of the network participants.
